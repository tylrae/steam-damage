How much have you actually spent on Steam?

Paste your purchase history and see your total, what each game cost you, and how much went on microtransactions. You get a set of cards to share at the end.

Is this safe to use?

Your history never leaves this tab. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and you don't log in here. If any page other than Steam asks for your Steam password, close it.

How can I check?

Copy your history from Steam, turn off your Wi-Fi, then paste it here. It still works, because nothing needs to go anywhere. On a computer you can also press F12, open the Network tab and paste: nothing gets sent.

If you can read code, the whole site is on GitHub. Those are the exact files this page runs, with no build step in between.

What ends up on my cards?

Copying the whole page also picks up your account details, wallet balance, how you paid and who you sent gifts to. This page ignores all of that. Your cards show game names, amounts and dates, and only leave your computer if you share them.

How to get your history

  1. 01
    Open your purchase history on Steam

    Sign in to Steam in your browser (not the Steam app), then open:

    store.steampowered.com/account/history↗

    Yes, Steam keeps every receipt. Every single one.

  2. 02
    Load all of it

    Scroll to the very bottom of the list (End jumps there) and click this button until it's gone:

    Load More Transactions Steam's button, pictured. Not part of this site.

    Long history? Settle in. We'll wait.

  3. 03
    Copy the whole page

    Ctrl+AthenCtrl+C

    Menus and all is fine. Only the table gets read.

  4. 04
    Come back and paste it anywhere here

    Ctrl+V

    Brace yourself.

FAQ

How do I see how much I've spent on Steam?

Steam has a page called "External Funds Used" (Help → Steam Support → My Account → Data Related to Your Steam Account) that shows one lifetime number. This site uses your purchase history instead, so you can see which games the money went to, how much was microtransactions, what you bought on sale, and more. Full guide →

Why doesn't my total match Steam's number?

Steam counts money coming into your account, including wallet top-ups. We add up what you actually bought, take off refunds, and convert other currencies at that year's average exchange rate. The two numbers are usually close, but rarely the same.

Does it work on my phone?

Not well. Copying your history from Steam is much easier on a computer, so start there.
